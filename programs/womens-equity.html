What we do

Ten routes to independence

01 — 10

Socio-Economic Upliftment

Providing financial literacy and sustainable income-generating opportunities.

Health & Hygiene Security

Comprehensive menstrual hygiene management to eliminate stigma.

Safety & Crisis Networks

Emergency crisis support services to protect women in distress.

Digital Commerce Integration

Training women in e-commerce to connect rural creators to global marketplaces.

Micro-Entrepreneurship

Transforming home-based skills into scalable, women-led enterprises.

Tribal Inclusivity

Programmes that honour, protect and monetise the traditional knowledge and skills of tribal women.

Modernized Agro-Processing

Food processing and value-addition units maximising profit from agricultural produce.

Tech-Driven Craft Centers

Tailoring, apparel and handicraft centres blending heritage design with contemporary global fashion.

End-to-End Skilling Ecosystems

Facilities housing physical skill labs and digital tech labs under one roof.

Community Leadership Pipelines

Mentoring grassroots women leaders to run SHGs, cooperatives and production centres independently.
