How We Measure

Our impact tracking is tied directly to our Root-to-Rise model, using data-driven metrics to ensure sustained growth and ultimate community independence.

Holistic Learning

Tracking student retention, skill acquisition, and transition to higher education.

Women's Equity

Measuring micro-enterprise revenue, financial independence, and community leadership roles.

Resilient Communities

Monitoring rural out-migration rates and localized economic growth.

Inclusive Wellness

Health screening coverage, disease prevention rates, and maternal health metrics.

Climate Resilience

Adoption rates of clean energy and disaster response readiness.

Eco-Conservation

Tracking native tree survival rates and wetland biodiversity health.
