AI Education for Home Tuition Teachers

Building on our foundation’s ongoing educational initiatives, we recently hosted our second workshop: a one-hour online session titled “AI Education for Home Tuition Teachers.” Designed to show private tutors how AI and digital tools can transform their daily workflow, the interactive session demonstrated practical ways to automate time-consuming administrative tasks such as lesson planning, worksheet creation and grading. By delegating routine preparation to AI, participating educators learned how to reclaim valuable time and redirect their energy toward what matters most: delivering personalised, high-impact instruction to their students.

Home tuition teachers often work alone, without the support staff or ready-made resources that schools provide. Every hour that goes into preparing material is an hour taken away from teaching. This workshop set out to give some of that time back.

What the session covered

  • Lesson planningTurning a topic and a class level into a structured lesson outline in minutes.
  • Worksheet creationGenerating practice questions and worksheets matched to each student’s level.
  • Grading supportUsing AI to speed up checking and feedback, so the tutor’s time goes into teaching.

What’s next

This workshop is part of our wider work on digital literacy. Next, we’re bringing AI assistants to more people in their own language, with upcoming sessions in Hindi and Marathi.
